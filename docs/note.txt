Death Note Overview

Death Note is a Japanese supernatural thriller manga written by Tsugumi Ohba and illustrated by Takeshi Obata. It was serialized in Weekly Shonen Jump from 2003 to 2006. The anime adaptation was produced by Madhouse and aired from 2006 to 2007, running for 37 episodes.

The story follows Light Yagami, a brilliant high school student who finds a notebook dropped by a Shinigami, a death god, named Ryuk. Anyone whose name is written in the Death Note while the writer pictures their face will die. Light decides to use the notebook to rid the world of criminals and create a new world with himself as its god, a persona the public comes to call Kira.

The international detective known only as L is brought in to catch Kira. L is eccentric, sits in an unusual crouched posture, eats large amounts of sweets, and rarely sleeps. The series is a cat-and-mouse battle of intellect between Light and L, with neither able to confirm the other's identity at first.

The Death Note comes with many rules, such as the requirement to write a cause of death within forty seconds of writing the name, or the person will die of a heart attack. The rules are central to the plot, because both Light and L exploit loopholes in them.

Ryuk is a Shinigami who is bored with the death god realm and drops the notebook into the human world out of curiosity. He enjoys watching events unfold and has a fondness for apples.

The series raises questions about justice, morality, and whether a person can be trusted with absolute power.
