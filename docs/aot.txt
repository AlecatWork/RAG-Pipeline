Attack on Titan Overview

Attack on Titan is a Japanese dark fantasy series created by Hajime Isayama. The manga began serialization in 2009 in Bessatsu Shonen Magazine and ran for twelve years, ending in 2021.

The story is set in a world where the last of humanity lives inside three concentric walls named Wall Maria, Wall Rose, and Wall Sina. The walls protect people from the Titans, giant humanoid creatures that devour humans for no obvious reason.

The main protagonist is Eren Yeager, who vows to destroy every Titan after his mother is killed during an attack on his home town. He joins the Survey Corps, a military branch that ventures beyond the walls to explore and fight Titans, alongside his childhood friends Mikasa Ackerman and Armin Arlert.

The anime adaptation premiered in 2013. Wit Studio produced the first three seasons, and MAPPA took over production for the fourth and final season, which aired from 2020 to 2023.

Captain Levi Ackerman is a fan-favorite character in the Survey Corps, widely regarded as humanity's strongest soldier. He is known for his exceptional skill with the omni-directional mobility gear, a harness system that lets soldiers swing through the air using grappling hooks and gas-powered propulsion.

The series is praised for its plot twists, which repeatedly change the audience's understanding of who the real enemy is and what lies beyond the walls.
